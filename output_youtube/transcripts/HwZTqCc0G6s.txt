Should you be using rag or should you be
using Obsidian with your cloud code
projects? Well, the answer is you
probably should be using Obsidian and
here's why.
For most people rag while awesome is
just completely overkill unless you have
something that you're working on that
has thousands and thousands of documents
that you need to call upon and get
answers about, Obsidian is probably more
than enough especially for just dealing
with basic text markdown files. Because
until you're at a crazy scale Obsidian
will work just fine for you and on top
of that it's infinitely easier to set up
and it's free. You can always move to a
rag system if your system gets too large
but at the beginning just stick with
Obsidian and if you need to shift to rag
it's going to be pretty obvious.
